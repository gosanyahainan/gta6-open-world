SOLMAR HEAT — a 2D top-down open-world street-crime game in the spirit of GTA 6. Explore the sunlit coastal city of Solmar on foot and by car: steal cars, outrun the Solmar PD through a five-star wanted system, and run five story jobs for the fixer Mara Solano — from a first pickup to the bank job that ends on a boat at the Marina. Traffic and pedestrians, a living minimap, fists and pistol combat, health and armour pickups, synthesized sound and music, and a local save. Pure static files: no build step, no network request, keyboard and mouse.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move or drive, E to enter or steal a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, M to start the next job, P to pause.
